## 5. Questions you should have asked

1. **"Walk me through the last time you split a utility bill. What did you actually do, step by step?"** This would tell you the real current habit and move "Did" from vague to specific.
2. **"How much is the bill, how much does your share change month to month, and have you ever paid for anything (an app, a service) to handle it?"** This would put a number on the cost and test whether "Paid" holds up.
3. **"Tell me about the last disagreement about the bill or about saving energy. What happened, and how did it end?"** This would turn "combative" into a concrete story with stakes.
